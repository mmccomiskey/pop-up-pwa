🎯 Group Crawl Rules & Wildcard Spots

  • Small bites & shared dishes: Don't order full sit-down entrees at each place — order 2-3 tacos or sausages to split among the crew so everyone stays agile.
  • Wildcard Option 1 — Alan The Pickle Guy (The Pickle Guys / LES): Mentioned in the chat near the Tenement Museum (Essex St). It's a bit further east, so keep it as a wildcard if the group decides to venture down to the Lower East Side. Google Maps Link
  • Wildcard Option 2 — Le Relais de Venise: Famous steak frites & secret green butter sauce (Midtown East on 52nd & Lex). Line starts before opening — great for another trip or if you decide to pivot east.
  • Wildcard Option 3 — Rétromobile Classic Car Show (Javits Center): 11th Ave & 34th St, directly adjacent to Hudson Yards if the crew still wants to pop in for collector car eye-candy.
